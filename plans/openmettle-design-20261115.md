# OpenMettle theme: making the art stand out

Research note for the OpenMettle theme in the family Open WebUI (15 November 2026). It records what current design-system guidance says about making brand art read well in a product UI, and how the theme applies it. Implementation and measurements: [OpenMettle theme](../docs/ASSISTANT.md#openmettle-theme).

## Principles

1. **One accent, used with intent.** Most of the interface stays neutral so the accent always points at something. Accent colours are used sparingly ([Refactoring UI, building a palette](https://refactoringui.com/previews/building-your-color-palette)); an illustration has one or two dominant colours plus small accents, and should not compete with the call to action ([Octopus design system, product illustrations](https://www.octopus.design/latest/brand/illustrations/product-illustrations-K2474yXx)). Pogton's system puts it in one line: one accent "so the blue always points at something" ([Pogton design system](https://pogton.com/design)).
2. **Neutrals with a temperature.** Greys do not have to be grey; warm or cool them toward the brand ([Refactoring UI, contents: "Greys don't have to be grey"](https://refactoringui.com/)). Avoid pure black: Material's dark theme uses dark grey surfaces, desaturated colours and lighter surfaces for elevation ([Google Design, Material dark theme](https://design.google/library/material-design-dark-theme); [Material 3 colour roles](https://m3.material.io/styles/color/roles)).
3. **Depth from light, not boxes.** Emulate one light source; lighter feels closer; a soft, wide shadow plus a tight contact shadow gives an object weight ([Refactoring UI, "Creating depth"](https://refactoringui.com/); [Smashing Magazine, shadows and blur](https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2017/02/shadows-blur-effects-user-interface-design/)). A mascot can sit in a "pool of light" with a contact shadow rather than inside a card ([Lockpaw DESIGN.md, elevation and light](https://github.com/sorkila/lockpaw/blob/main/DESIGN.md)).
4. **One focal point and room around it.** Hierarchy comes as much from de-emphasising competitors as from emphasis, and negative space lets the hero read ([Refactoring UI, "De-emphasize to emphasize"](https://refactoringui.com/); [Siemens Element, illustration principles](https://github.com/siemens/element/blob/main/docs/fundamentals/illustrations.md)).
5. **Art that survives both themes.** Put art on or over solid fills so it stays visible on any background ([GitLab Pajamas, creating illustrations](https://design.gitlab.com/product-foundations/illustration-creation-guide/)); check illustrations in light and dark and meet contrast where the art carries meaning ([Siemens Element](https://github.com/siemens/element/blob/main/docs/fundamentals/illustrations.md)). In dark mode, soften bright backgrounds so art does not glow ([Apple HIG, Dark Mode](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/dark-mode)).
6. **Small marks are drawn for their size.** A logo that blurs at 16 px needs a version tailored to the small pixel grid ([Evil Martians, How to favicon](https://evilmartians.com/chronicles/how-to-favicon-in-2021-six-files-that-fit-most-needs/)); a dark-mode tab icon needs strong contrast against both tab colours ([Faviconry, dark-mode favicons](https://ozgur.ca/project/faviconry/guide/dark-mode-favicon)). Maskable install icons keep the subject in the central safe zone.
7. **Motion is restrained and optional.** Subtle motion can add warmth, but non-essential animation must respect the reduced-motion preference ([W3C, Understanding SC 2.3.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html)). No loops, no attention-seeking pulses.
8. **Contrast is not optional.** Text at 4.5:1, interface graphics and focus indicators at 3:1 (WCAG 2.x 1.4.3, 1.4.11); Apple asks for at least 4.5:1 and recommends 7:1 for small custom-coloured text ([Apple HIG, Dark Mode](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/dark-mode)).

## How the theme applies them

| Principle | In the theme |
| --- | --- |
| One accent | Vermilion only for the send button, the "Mettle" in the wordmark, focus outlines, selected borders and links. Open WebUI's blue and sky become the same vermilion scale; green success, red error and amber warning keep their meaning |
| Neutrals with a temperature | Open WebUI's grey scale retinted: ivory at the light end (#FAF7F1, #F2EBDD), graphite at the dark end (#24272C, #191C21). The Family AI greens are gone from sidebar, settings and sign-in |
| Depth from light | On the new-chat screen Mettle sits in a soft pool of light (white in light mode, faint ivory and vermilion in dark mode) with a contact shadow beneath; the sign-in backdrop uses the same warm glow |
| One focal point | The welcome shows Mettle at 128 px with space around him; when the only model wears the default icon, the small copy beside the model name is hidden so the mark appears once |
| Art in both themes | Small icons sit on a solid ivory tile (not white, so they do not glare in dark mode); the tab icons use the bare head, whose ivory rim and red tab read on light and dark tabs |
| Small marks | Icons start from a tight crop of the head (the original carries about 27% empty margin); tiles keep the head inside the circle that avatars crop to, maskable icons inside the 80% safe zone |
| Restrained motion | One 420 ms fade-and-rise as the welcome appears, only when the browser reports no reduced-motion preference; nothing loops |
| Contrast | Measured pairs are listed in the docs; light mode darkens Open WebUI's secondary greys so they reach 4.5:1 on white |
